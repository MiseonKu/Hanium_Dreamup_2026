# Collected Static-Trial Data Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-18. This document records source-selection evidence before
model training; it is not a model-performance report.

## Which Acquisition Is Usable

`07_WT901C` is excluded from supervised v1 preparation. Its three GUI modes
record continuous four-IMU JSON, but they do not provide synchronized Vive
endpoint labels or settled-trial identity, and the encoder source in its
encoder-trigger mode is an adapter placeholder.

The usable source is the 08 `real_motion_demo` acquisition generated by
`experiment_scripts/collect_3d_sweep_50_v2.py`. That distinction matters:
`SessionController` did not drive the real experiment. The tracked 06
`derived_dataset_archive.zip` is a versioned snapshot of the rebuildable 08
derivation and is read without accessing hardware or ignored raw sessions.

## Warning-Only Quality Findings

Among the 7,707 canonical rows:

- 10 have tip-IMU gyro RMS above `0.02 rad/s`;
- 23 have at least one IMU gyro RMS above `0.02 rad/s`;
- 8 have at least one Vive position-axis standard deviation above `1 mm`;
- 7 have at least one encoder standard deviation above `1 tick`.

These statistics do not match the rolling settling detector one-to-one and do
not justify deleting labels. V1 records them as warnings and lets later
training compare a strict-QC sensitivity slice against the full eligible
population.

The warning-free strict-QC slice contains 7,670 rows, 32 fewer than the full
eligible cohort. Its median within-`(segment,target)` 3D repeatability standard
deviation is 18.798 mm versus 19.098 mm for the full cohort (change -0.300 mm).
Its median between-segment target-centroid drift is 21.196 mm versus 21.194 mm
(change +0.002 mm). The small changes are reported as sensitivity evidence,
not used to hard-delete warning rows.

Within `(segment,target)` cells of the eligible population, median measured
axis standard deviations are approximately 8.275 mm (Vive X), 15.526 mm
(Vive Y), and 6.777 mm (Vive Z); their 95th percentiles are 12.580, 28.276,
and 13.750 mm. These repeated-trial spreads show why row-random evaluation
would be optimistic and why segment and target groups must remain intact.

## Why `timeseries.parquet` Is Excluded

The existing 08 v1 timeseries builder is useful as a rebuildable exploratory
working layer, but it is not the source for this release. Static inspection
found that it places a uniform 50 Hz grid over roughly 10 Hz per-packet IMU
data using nearest-sample selection, can choose a future sample, repeats source
values without a source-age/missingness contract, and has trial/block/drop
provenance inconsistencies. This combination can hide stale/repeated data and
mix rows that the trial-level contract rejects.

No claim is made that time-series learning is impossible. It requires a new
contract with causal or explicitly bounded alignment, source timestamps/ages,
missingness masks, drop propagation, and group-first evaluation. That work is
outside `alphaf.ml_preparation.v1`.

## Leakage And Shortcut Findings

- The legacy `q_encoder_rad_j*` and `logical_deg_j*` fields encode historical
  semantic assumptions. Physical encoder delta is recomputed directly from
  raw ticks and documented references.
- Planned XYZ is a command/target descriptor strongly tied to repeated target
  identity. It is never a primary feature; it is retained only for admissible
  neighbor constraints and an explicitly named ablation.
- Vive position/quaternion, tracking quality, and label-derived distances are
  prohibited from deployable feature views. C-Mixup may use label distance to
  choose two **training** parents, as its algorithm requires, but never sees a
  held-out label.
- An optimal rigid alignment fitted on all rows would expose held-out label
  geometry. V1 preserves raw `vive_world` millimetres and performs no Kabsch
  fit.
- Augmentation must occur after a segment/target outer split. Synthetic rows
  are training-only and retain both real parent IDs so this can be audited.

split assignments, and full parent provenance.

## Remaining Measurement Limits

- WT901C full-scale register settings were not read back during acquisition.
- The units did not output a verifiable quaternion packet; Euler axis/order
  convention therefore remains unverified.
- Magnetometers are not calibrated and are ablation-only.
- Tracker-to-robot-base/TCP extrinsic calibration and tracker offset are not
  independently measured. Raw Vive world position is a label, not a robot-base
  coordinate claim.
- Four sensor summaries across the endpoint rig are not the formal
  three-IMU-per-link ShapeFK arrangement.
- V1 prepares data only. It makes no accuracy, generalization, causal, or
  physical-deformation claim.
